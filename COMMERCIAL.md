# Open source and commercial products

Syrebrum Core is licensed under Apache-2.0. Everything in this repository is
free to use, change and redistribute under that license, including in
commercial products. There is no paid tier of Core and no license key.

CCD Enterprise & Development LLC also sells separate products, such as the
Syrebrum app. They are distributed under their own terms and are not part of
this repository. Using Core doesn't require any of them, and nothing here
calls a hosted CCD service.

Anyone may build a FailStop engine, model adapter or other extension against
the documented interfaces in [docs/SPEC.md](docs/SPEC.md), under any license.
